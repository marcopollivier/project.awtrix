Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

project.awtrix is a personal, non-commercial project that shows the owner's own social media follower counts on a desk LED clock (Ulanzi TC001). It is not offered to the public.

What is read

With your authorization through TikTok Login Kit, the app reads only your follower count (scope user.info.stats) and the basic profile identifier TikTok requires (user.info.basic). It does not read videos, messages, likes or any other content, and it never posts anything.

Where it is stored

The follower count and the access tokens are stored only on the clock and, optionally, on the owner's own computer. They are not sent to any server operated by this project, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising or analytics.

Revoking access

You can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services. Revoking stops all reads immediately.
